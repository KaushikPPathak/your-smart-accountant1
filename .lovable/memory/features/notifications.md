---
name: Notifications scope
description: Pop-up reminders are for return-filing deadlines only and must obey the reminders switch
type: preference
---
Pop-up notifications (and the morning summary's alerts) show only return-filing deadlines (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, TDS for audit cases).
Never pop up negative stock, duplicate voucher, MSME, GST hygiene or balance alerts.
When the company's reminders switch is off, no reminders appear and no background scan runs.
**Why:** user found the extra alerts noisy and they froze the app.
